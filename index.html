Log Cardio / Run

1. Pigeon Pose

Bring one knee forward and angle it toward your wrist while extending the back leg straight behind you. Keep hips square and gently fold forward.

Hold 60–90 seconds per leg (Glutes & Rotators)

2. Couch Stretch

Place your back knee against a wall or couch with your shin vertical, stepping your opposite foot forward into a deep lunge.

Hold 60 seconds per leg (Hip Flexors & Quads)

3. 90/90 Hip Switches

Sit on the floor with both knees bent at 90-degree angles—one leg internally rotated in front, the other externally behind. Smoothly swivel knees to the opposite side.

10–12 smooth alternating reps (Internal/External Rotation)

4. Butterfly Stretch

Sit tall with the soles of your feet pressed together and knees dropped open toward the floor. Gently hinge forward from your hips.

Hold 90 seconds (Adductors & Groin for Middle Splits)

5. Active Front Split Slider

Kneel on a sliding disc or towel, slowly sliding your front leg forward into a split while keeping your torso upright and hands supported on blocks or the floor.

3 sets × 5 slow eccentric lowerings

6. Lizard Pose

From a deep runner's lunge, lower your forearms toward the floor inside your front foot while keeping your back leg active and straight.

Hold 60 seconds per leg (Deep Hip Flexors & Groin)

7. Seated Straddle Pancake Stretch

Sit with legs spread wide in a V-shape, toes pointed upward. Keep spine neutral and walk hands forward between your legs.

Hold 60–90 seconds (Adductors & Lower Back)

8. Kneeling Hamstring Stretch

Kneel on one knee with the opposite leg extended straight forward on your heel. Hinge forward from your hips with a flat back.

Hold 45–60 seconds per leg (Hamstrings)

9. Frog Pose

Get on your hands and knees, widen your knees as far as comfortable, keeping ankles in line with knees, and gently rock your hips back and forth.

Hold 60–90 seconds (Adductors & Groin)

10. Cossack Squat Hold

Step wide into a deep side lunge, keeping the working heel flat on the floor while toes point forward or slightly out. Hold at the bottom for deep adductor and ankle mobility.

8–10 reps per side
