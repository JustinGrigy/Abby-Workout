Hip Mobility & Splits Routine

Daily mobility protocol to unlock deep hip opening and splits.

🕊️
#1 Pigeon Pose

Hip Rotators & Glute Opener

Bring front shin forward, square hips, fold forward gently. Hold 60-90s per leg to open hip rotators.

🛋️
#2 Couch Stretch

Deep Hip Flexor & Quad Length

Back knee against wall, upright torso, squeeze glute. Hold 60s per leg.

🔄
#3 90/90 Hip Switches

Internal & External Rotation

Seated with legs at 90-degree angles, smoothly rotate knees side to side. 10-12 reps.

🦋
#4 Butterfly Stretch

Adductor & Groin Opener

Soles of feet together, knees dropping toward floor, hinge forward. Hold 90s.

📐
#5 Active Front Split Slider

Progressive Eccentric Lowering

Front heel on slider/towel, slowly slide into split extension. 3 sets x 5 slow reps.
